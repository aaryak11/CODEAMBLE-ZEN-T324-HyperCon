# HyperCon — 3-Minute Demo Pitch Script
**Team KAMPASTRA | ZEN-T324**

---

### [0:00 - 0:40] The Problem & The Solution
**Speaker:** 
"Good morning judges. We are Team KAMPASTRA. Have you ever ordered fresh groceries online, only to receive bruised tomatoes and wilted spinach? The core problem with online grocery shopping is that it is *blind*. You trust a dark warehouse with your daily food quality.

Our solution is **HyperCon**. Instead of warehouses, HyperCon connects you directly to local neighborhood stores. But we don't just show you an inventory list—we provide **Live Shelf Camera Feeds** paired with **Edge AI Computer Vision** and **Natural Language LLM Search**. We bring the transparency of physically walking into a store, directly to your screen."

### [0:40 - 1:15] Demo Flow: AI Natural Language Search (Groq Llama 3.3)
*(Action: Open the app, type in Hindi/Hinglish "sabji 100 rupees mein" or "fresh tomato chahiye" in the search bar, and hit search.)*

**Speaker:** 
"Let's look at a live demo. Notice our search bar powered by Groq and Llama 3.3. I can search conversationally in Hindi or Hinglish, like *'sabji 100 rupees mein'*. 

Watch how HyperCon instantly interprets the intent in sub-50 milliseconds—extracting the budget limit of ₹100, filtering for fresh vegetables, and prioritizing the freshest, closest neighborhood stores with our composite Trust Score."

### [1:15 - 2:00] Demo Flow: Live Shelf Stream & Real Computer Vision (TensorFlow.js)
*(Action: Click 'Live View' on a produce item or click a green store pin on the Interactive Map.)*

**Speaker:** 
"Now for the magic of HyperCon. Before I add this to my cart, I am looking at a live camera feed of the actual physical shelf in the store. 

Notice these green bounding boxes tracking the produce in real-time. This isn't a pre-rendered video overlay—we are running **TensorFlow.js and MobileNet COCO-SSD directly in the browser**, running client-side inference on the live video stream. It identifies stock levels, classifies produce, and verifies camera uptime and stream authenticity with zero latency."

### [2:00 - 2:40] Demo Flow: Geospatial Intelligence & Verification
*(Action: Show the Interactive Store Map with Trust Badges and live feed status.)*

**Speaker:** 
"HyperCon gives customers complete geospatial awareness. Our map shows precisely where stores are located, color-coded by the health of their live feed. Green means verified and authentic. Our background AI verification worker continuously scans for motion and frame tampering to ensure no video loops or spoofed feeds."

### [2:40 - 3:00] Demo Flow: Checkout & Conclusion
*(Action: Click 'Add to Cart', open the Cart Drawer, and click 'Proceed to Checkout'.)*

**Speaker:** 
"Satisfied with the verified produce quality, I add it to my cart and proceed to our instant checkout with zero platform markup. 

In conclusion, HyperCon isn't just an e-commerce app. It's a trust engine for local retail powered by real edge AI and computer vision. Thank you, and we'd love to take your questions."
